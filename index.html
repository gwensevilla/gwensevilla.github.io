What I do

I wear a lot of hats. Here's what that looks like.

On any given week I might be editing a podcast episode, updating a website, building a funnel, or managing an email campaign. Executive Support

Calendar and email management, research, documentation, and project coordination. I handle the day-to-day so you can focus on the bigger picture.

Podcast Production

Partnerships & Coordination

Managing joint venture partnerships, speaker outreach, publishing schedules, and client communication. I keep relationships and timelines on track behind the scenes.

About me

A little more about who I am.


I'm Gwen, a freelance executive assistant and digital operations specialist based in Cebu, Philippines. I started my career in tech support and customer care, spent 7 years managing operations for a global web hosting company, and have been working remotely as a freelance EA since 2022. I'm currently pursuing my MBA.

Outside of work I'm a parent, a tennis trainee, and a hobbyist guitarist. I come from a sports background, former varsity table tennis player, and have also done swimming, softball, and taekwondo. That discipline and competitive mindset carries over into how I approach my work.

I work with entrepreneurs who need someone reliable in the background. Someone who can figure things out, keep things moving, and genuinely care about the outcome.
